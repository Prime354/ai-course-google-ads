Frequently Asked Questions

01 What is the AI Mastery Course?

The AI Mastery Course is a practical training program designed to help you use AI for content creation, video advertisements, voice calling agents, WhatsApp automation, business workflows, and AI-powered digital solutions.

02 Who can join this course?

This course is suitable for students, beginners, freelancers, working professionals, business owners, digital marketers, content creators, and aspiring AI agency owners who want to develop practical AI skills.

03 Do I need prior AI knowledge or coding experience?

No prior AI knowledge or advanced coding experience is required. You will learn AI concepts and practical applications step by step, starting with the fundamentals.

04 What will I learn in the AI Mastery Course?

You will master hands-on, high-value workflows for autonomous business automation, 24/7 AI voice calling agents, cinematic video advertising, intelligent messaging systems, and high-income freelancing and agency operations.

05 Will I work on practical projects?

Yes. The course focuses on practical projects based on real-world applications, helping you develop useful skills and build a portfolio to demonstrate your capabilities.

06 Can this course help me start freelancing?

Yes. You will explore AI-based services you can offer to potential clients, learn to develop practical solutions, and understand how to present your services professionally. Your success depends on your skills, portfolio, and ability to find and serve clients.

07 Can I start or grow an AI agency after this course?

The course introduces AI service opportunities, business automation, client communication, and the fundamentals of building an AI agency. You will learn how to identify potential business needs and develop AI-based solutions. Business growth and income are not guaranteed.

08 Is this course suitable for business owners and working professionals?

Yes. You will explore how AI can support marketing, lead generation, customer communication, content production, and repetitive business tasks to improve productivity and streamline workflows.

09 What is the course duration and learning format?

The course duration is approximately 2–3 months, with a hybrid learning format combining online and offline learning. Contact Prime Academy for the latest batch schedule and available options.

10 Will I receive a certificate after completing the course?

Yes. You will receive a course completion certificate, subject to the academy's course completion requirements.
